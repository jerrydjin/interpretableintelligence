What does it see?

CLIP, a model that matches pictures to words, running in your browser. Pick a picture and it tells you what it sees. Then find the features inside it that make it see that, and switch them off.

Getting the model ready.

Drop a picture anywhere

Runs on your device with ONNX Runtime Web; your pictures never leave it. Model openai/clip-vit-base-patch16 (MIT), read at block 8 of 12 through a dictionary of 6,144 features trained on photos and robot video. The words are a list of everyday things; the model scores each as "a photo of a …". "Why it sees" ranks features by a first-order estimate (activation times gradient), leaving out a few always-on features that move any word; switching them off is the real test, and the random line beside it is the control. This is exploration, not a verified finding.

Prefer to browse every feature? The feature playground runs DINOv2 on robot video.
