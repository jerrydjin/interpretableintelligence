Stop rule

Stop rule: Sobel–Wald. Stop when the rise test or the fall test accepts H1, or both accept H0, or at 75 episodes. Rise compares 0.56 with 0.86. Fall compares 0.56 with 0.26. Alpha is 0.05. Beta is 0.05. 0.56 is the capture success rate, 56/100. The verdict is rise when the rise test accepts H1, fall when the fall test accepts H1, and no_effect when both accept H0. At the cap with no decision, the verdict is cap_undecided.

Method checks

Reconstruction only: the hook replaced block 5 with the SAE reconstruction and edited no latent. Actions were identical to baseline in 8 of 8 shared-seed episodes, success matched in 8 of 8, and the largest change at the hook was 0. So the hook itself does not move the policy.

SAE: BatchTopK, k=32, 8192 latents, trained on block 5 of the 100-episode capture (56 successes). Held-out explained variance 0.998 on 25 held-out episodes (22208 tokens). loss_recovered: not measured.

Failure labels: the first labeller judged a drop on whichever object was nearest the gripper, and marked pot 1's normal placement on the stove as a drop in 37 of 40 cases. Failure onset was re-derived from pot 0 (rule pot0-v1) before the picker chose candidates.
