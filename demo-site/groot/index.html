GR00T N1.7 on LIBERO

Feature 720 tracked failures, and turning it off dropped success from 39/75 to 1/9. Turning off random features caused falls too in 5 of 20 runs. `ii` makes you run that control before calling a feature real.

641 rollouts. The matched random-feature controls that caught this result live in the repo's ablation runner, src/interpretableintelligence/adapters/groot/v9/ablate_v9.py, not through an ii command. That is the check ii is built around, until GR00T ablation is wired into ii.

Tested: no causal effect found

The test was whether switching a feature off raises success. Neither candidate did, so both are real=false.

Baseline: 39/75 (52%), Wilson 95% interval [0.4087, 0.6293].

Feature 720: 1/9, verdict fall.

The 7081+5852 pair: 23/63 (37%), verdict fall.

5 of the 20 random controls also fell, so a fall is not specific to these features.

Method checks

Reconstruction only: the hook replaced block 5 with the SAE reconstruction and edited no latent. Actions were identical to baseline in 8 of 8 shared-seed episodes, success matched in 8 of 8, and the largest change at the hook was 0. So the hook itself does not move the policy.

SAE: BatchTopK, k=32, 8192 latents, trained on block 5 of the 100-episode capture (56 successes). Held-out explained variance 0.998 on 25 held-out episodes (22208 tokens). loss_recovered: not measured.

Failure labels: the first labeller judged a drop on whichever object was nearest the gripper, and marked pot 1's normal placement on the stove as a drop in 37 of 40 cases. Failure onset was re-derived from pot 0 (rule pot0-v1) before the picker chose candidates.

Sources: demo-data/groot/recon-only-summary.json, demo-data/groot/sae-heldout-eval.json, adapters/groot/v9/V9_NOTES.md.

Conditions share seeds. Paired change is against baseline on the shared seeds. From demo-data/groot/groot-1028-ablate-summary.json.

The Wilson interval assumes the number of episodes was fixed in advance. Each SPRT condition stopped as soon as its boundary was crossed, so the sequential column is the exact 95% interval under that stopping rule, valid however early the run stopped. It is usually wider, and it is the one to read for the ablated conditions.
