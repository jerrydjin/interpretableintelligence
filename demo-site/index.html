Ask a vision model what it sees, then find out why.

Pick a picture and the model says what it sees, in words. ii shows you the features inside it that make it see that, and lets you switch them off to check.

Open the playgroundRun it on your model

  • PlaygroundYour own pictures, in your browser. See what the model sees, which features make it see that, and switch them off.
  • Your modelThe same tools on any Hugging Face vision or video model, on your laptop.
  • V-JEPA 2A robot's world model: its scored features, with where each fires and which checks it passed.

Research

A feature tracked our robot's failures. The control said it wasn't the cause. Switching off feature 720 in NVIDIA's GR00T dropped success from 39/75 to 1/9. Random features did that too. How ii kept us from calling it a finding.

ii is free, local and open on GitHub.
